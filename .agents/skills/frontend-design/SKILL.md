# Frontend Design
Design from the product domain rather than a template. Avoid common AI tells: identical rounded SaaS cards, decorative gradient washes, tracked all-caps eyebrow labels everywhere, mono meta strings used as decoration, and near-black-plus-neon palettes without product rationale. Typography, layout and interaction must have a specific point of view tied to the task.
